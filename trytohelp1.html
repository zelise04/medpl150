You Try to Help

You rush into the living room, putting a hand over your mouth to try to block some of the particulates from aggravating your lungs even further.

You start to kick the rubble and remove some with your free hand. As you keep rummaging, you feel something soft and faintly breathing.


As you continue to move the debris, you know that this thing is not human… but it also doesn’t look like any animal you’ve seen before.




Though, it kind of looks…cute?
