Reflection

I really enjoyed the process of taking images around campus. I buddied up with a freshman and it was fun to show her some hidden gems on campus. I feel like being in such a sometimes stressful environment can cause one to be blind to the beauty around you. Even though Hunter’s campus does have its faults, especially seeing the recent pipe bursts, there is a lot of underappreciated beauty in it. I had some trouble adjusting the aperture and had to look up the camera’s user guide to figure it out. I also had to get used to the awkwardness of walking around with a decently sized camera around my neck.

Photoshop was the most difficult aspect of this assignment. It is something that I am completely new to. It took me some time to even figure out how to correctly crop my image without losing the parts of the images I wanted to focus on. The LinkedIn learning lessons were very helpful with this. I actually did not face too many issues with the adjustment layer aspect of the assignment. I did, however, stick to using levels instead of curves because I was a bit intimidated by curves when watching the video. In the future, I do want to use curves more as it does give a lot more autonomy in adjusting the tone of the image.
